Enables militia grunt rebreather masks when playing on Runoff and Sandtrap, randomizes them on other maps

Militia grunts have a "Rebreather Mask" bodygroup, that gives them a small facemask. This bodygroup is normally turned on for ALL militia grunts when playing on Outpost 207, likely indicating that the map has no breathable atmosphere. This mod just enables that bodygroup on other maps as well.

Runoff is pretty obvious as to why (its covered in shit water everywhere), while Sandtrap seemingly takes place in another moon (maybe the same one as Outpost? At the very least its pretty close)
